Why Choose Pit Lane

A shop that still does the work by hand

Get the car clean, put right what can be put right, then protect it.

01. HAND WORK ONLY
No Brush Machines

Every vehicle is washed by hand with pH-neutral snow foam and clean microfiber mitts to prevent swirl marks.

02. PRICE BEFORE WORK
Transparent Quotes

Every car is inspected before a number is given. You hear the exact price before the work starts, never after.

03. FAST TURNAROUND
20 to 30 Mins Wash

Efficient team coordination ensures a thorough hand wash in 20–30 minutes without pushing your car aside.

04. TWO LANGUAGES
English & 中文 Service

Come in speaking either English or Chinese. The quote, the explanation and the receipt read clearly in both.

✗ We will not polish a dirty car

Nothing gets polished, waxed or coated until the paint is fully decontaminated and tree sap or road tar are dissolved off. Skipping that saves an hour and ruins the paint.

✗ We will not promise what paint cannot give

A deep scratch through the clear coat will not vanish by magic. We inspect paint depth first and tell you honestly at the counter how much better it can get.
